## 🙋 About Me

I'm a data analyst with **1.5+ years of professional experience** building **Power BI** dashboards and data models that organizations actually use to make decisions — not reports that get opened once and forgotten.

My work sits at the intersection of **business intelligence** and **predictive analytics**: I don't just show what happened, I build toward what's *about* to happen — using **DAX**, **Power Query**, and **machine learning (Random Forest, scikit-learn)** to turn dashboards from descriptive into predictive.

Domains I've worked across: **customer retention, financial reporting, transaction analytics, and customer segmentation.**
