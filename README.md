## 🧩 Project Summaries

### 🧾 Churn Analysis Project
- **Objective:** Analyze and predict customer churn to improve retention rates.  
- **Key Outcomes:** Identified churn factors such as contract type, tenure, and customer service usage.  
- **Highlights:** KPI creation using DAX, trend analysis, and interactive retention visuals.  

---

### 💳 Credit Card Analysis Project
- **Objective:** Explore and visualize credit card transaction patterns.  
- **Key Outcomes:** Revealed customer segments driving revenue growth and spending trends.  
- **Highlights:** Dynamic drill-throughs, time intelligence DAX measures, and profitability dashboards.  

---

### 📈 RFM Analysis Project
- **Objective:** Segment customers based on Recency, Frequency, and Monetary metrics and predict high-value and churn-risk customers using **Random Forest ML**.  
- **Key Outcomes:** Enabled targeted marketing strategies by identifying high-value and at-risk customer clusters.  
- **Highlights:** RFM scoring logic with DAX, ML-driven predictions with Random Forest, segmentation visuals, and actionable insights.  

---

### 💰 Financial Performance Dashboard
- **Objective:** Deliver a comprehensive financial analytics solution that enables organizations to understand overall performance across sales, profit, COGS, and product margins.  
- **Key Outcomes:** Provided clear visibility into month-by-month trends, year-over-year performance shifts, regional profitability differences, and product-level margin behavior. The dashboard also reveals the impact of discounts on profit, highlights high and low-margin products, and uncovers countries contributing the most or least to total profitability.  
- **Highlights:** Built a multi-page interactive dashboard featuring an executive financial overview, detailed product and segment profitability analysis, country-level performance insights, and a dynamic What-If simulation model that helps evaluate margin improvement scenarios and strategic financial decisions.
